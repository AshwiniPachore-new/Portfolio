Dairy Management Project

Dairy Management Project

Frameworks Used: Node.js with Express.js for backend and Flutter for frontend. The database used is MongoDB.

Animal Management
  • Record and track details of animals (e.g., ID, breed, age, health status).
  • Maintain vaccination and medical records.
  • Monitor milk production by animal.
Milk Collection & Distribution
  • Log daily milk production.
  • Track milk quality (fat percentage, SNF levels).
  • Manage distribution to customers or vendors.
Feed Management
  • Record feed inventory and consumption.
  • Track feeding schedules and nutritional needs for animals.
Employee Management
  • Manage employee details and roles.
  • Track attendance and payroll.
Financial Management
  • Maintain sales and expense records.
  • Generate invoices for customers.
  • Monitor profits, losses, and other financial metrics.
Inventory Management
  • Track supplies like feed, medicines, and packaging materials.
  • Set alerts for low stock levels.
Customer and Vendor Management
  • Record customer and vendor details.
  • Handle orders and payments.
Reporting & Analytics
  • Generate reports on milk production, sales, and animal health.
  • Analyze trends in revenue and productivity.
Baap Accounting Project

Baap Accounting Project

Frameworks Used: Node.js with Express.js for backend and React.js for frontend. The database used is PostgreSQL.

Core Features
  • Manage the entire company's accounts, including income, expenses, and budgets.
  • Automate recurring financial tasks like payroll and bills.
  • Generate detailed financial statements and tax reports.
Invoicing
  • Generate professional invoices for clients and vendors.
  • Track payment statuses and overdue payments.
Financial Reporting
  • Provide real-time insights into company finances.
  • Analyze trends in revenue and expenses.
  • Customizable dashboard for key financial metrics.
Multi-user Support
  • Allow multiple users with role-based access to accounts.
  • Secure login, session management, and activity logging.
Integration
  • Integrate with bank accounts for real-time transaction updates.
  • Support for third-party payroll and tax systems.
Baap Voting Project

Baap Voting Project

Frameworks Used: Fastify.js for backend and Flutter for frontend. The database used is mysql.

Core Features
  • Secure online voting platform for elections.
  • Role-based access for administrators, candidates, and voters.
  • Real-time vote counting and result generation.
Voter Management
  • Register and verify voters using unique IDs.
  • Prevent duplicate voting using secure token-based systems.
  • Manage voter details, including name, age, and address.
Candidate Management
  • Register candidates with details like name, party, and manifesto.
  • Allow voters to view candidate profiles before voting.
Election Management
  • Create and manage multiple elections.
  • Set start and end times for voting periods.
  • Enable voting for specific regions or groups.
Security Features
  • End-to-end encryption for all votes.
  • Audit logs to track voting activities.
  • Two-factor authentication for administrators and voters.
Reporting & Analytics
  • Generate detailed reports for election results.
  • Visualize voting trends and voter turnout.
  • Export data in multiple formats (CSV, PDF).

Baap Good People Foundation

Baap Good People Foundation Project

Frameworks Used: Fastify.ts for backend and React for frontend. The database used is mysql.

Purpose
  • Empower elderly people and young children by providing opportunities to earn while learning new skills.
  • Support underprivileged communities through skill development and mentorship programs.
Key Features
  • Create and manage profiles for elderly individuals and children.
  • Offer skill-based training programs tailored to individual needs.
  • Connect participants with job opportunities and internships.
Community Engagement
  • Organize workshops and events to promote awareness and inclusivity.
  • Facilitate collaboration between volunteers and participants.
Reporting & Monitoring
  • Track participant progress and success stories.
  • Generate detailed reports on community impact and engagement metrics.
